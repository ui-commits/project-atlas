Oswego Park is a forest-intelligence design system engineered for high-density information displays and low-light operational environments.

### Highlights

- **60+ Core Components:** From dense data tables and telemetry meters to custom iconographic glyphs.
- **Theme Matrix:** 4 calibrated palettes designed around field survey and wilderness telemetry.
- **Micro-Interactions:** Subtle, non-intrusive feedback states designed for prolonged monitoring sessions.
