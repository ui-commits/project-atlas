RUNTIMEDISTR (Hermes C2) explores hierarchical infrastructure ontologies through synchronized projections across geographic, topology, hardware, ontology, and knowledge-space dimensions.

### Core Capabilities

- **Multi-Projection Synchronization:** Simultaneous geographic map, topology graph, and hardware breakdown views linked to a shared selection state.
- **Command Palette & Telemetry:** Rapid keyboard-first navigation with real-time stream inspection and alert thresholds.
- **Ontology Explorer:** Interactive node drill-down tracking parent-child infrastructure relationships.
